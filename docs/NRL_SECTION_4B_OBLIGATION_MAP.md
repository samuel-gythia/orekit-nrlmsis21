# `NRL-SOF-014-1` Section-by-Section Obligation Map (`orekit-nrlmsis21`)

This attachment maps the exact text of [`python/orekit_nrlmsis21/nrlmsis2.1_license.txt`](../python/orekit_nrlmsis21/nrlmsis2.1_license.txt) (`MSIS® (NRL-SOF-014-1) SOFTWARE OPEN SOURCE ACADEMIC RESEARCH LICENSE AGREEMENT`) to `orekit-nrlmsis21`, distinguishing obligations that are clearly established from questions that require written NRL clarification.

---

## 1. Section 1 — Agreement

> *"1. Agreement. The MSIS® empirical atmospheric model software (hereinafter “Software”) is property of The Government of the United States of America. This software is being made available under the following terms and conditions. By using, modifying, reproducing, or preparing a derivative work of this Software, you agree to abide by the terms and conditions herein."*

- **Relevance**: Any party that uses, compiles, modifies, reproduces, or prepares a derivative work of NRLMSIS 2.1 becomes bound by the agreement.
- **Clarification Needed**: Whether distributing pure wrapper layers (`native/nrlmsis21_jni.c`, `java/`, `python/`) *without* upstream NRLMSIS 2.1 files constitutes "preparing a derivative work of this Software" when `native/nrlmsis21_c_api.F90` is designed to `USE` NRLMSIS 2.1 modules at user build time.

---

## 2. Section 2 — Permitted Purposes & Commercial-Use Restriction

> *"2. License. In accordance with federal law, authorization is given to use, reproduce, and modify the Software solely for research, academic, and non-profit purposes and only in accordance with the terms and conditions in this Agreement. Any commercial use is prohibited. No other rights or permissions are provided."*

- **Clearly Established**:
  - End users running `orekit-nrlmsis21` with the `NRL-SOF-014-1` release of NRLMSIS 2.1 are restricted strictly to **research, academic, and non-profit purposes**.
  - **Any commercial use is prohibited** under `NRL-SOF-014-1`.
- **Clarification Needed**:
  - If a commercial user obtains a separate commercial license for NRLMSIS 2.1 directly from NRL, may that user combine their licensed NRLMSIS 2.1 source with independently authored `orekit-nrlmsis21` wrapper layers?

## 4. Section 4(a) — Sales, Licensing & Transfers for a Fee

> *"a. Sales. A user of the Software shall not sell, or license, or transfer for a fee the Software or portion thereof, or any derivative work of the Software, or any data products generated by the Software, without first obtaining the written consent of IP Counsel for the Naval Research Laboratory."*

- **Clearly Established**:
  - Neither upstream NRLMSIS 2.1, any derivative work, nor **any data products generated by the Software** may be sold, licensed, or transferred for a fee without prior written consent from NRL IP Counsel.
- **Clarification Needed**:
  - What is the NRL IP Counsel contact and procedure for organizations seeking commercial licensing or paid data-product consent?
  - Does publishing open academic benchmark datasets or orbit-determination validation results (for free, without fee) implicate any restriction?

---

## 5. Section 4(b) — Modifications, Derivative Works, Notices, Delivery & Open-Source Obligations

> *"b. Modifications. All modifications to the Software and derivative works of the Software (including translations to other programming languages) shall carry prominent notices stating how the files were changed and the date of the change. Any party who modifies the Software shall deliver the modified portion of the Software to authors or NRL Code 7630, U.S. Naval Research Laboratory. Any reproductions, modified versions of the Software, or derivative works shall be made available to the public as open source software. A party who modifies the Software or creates a derivative work of the Software hereby grants the Government of the United States of America a non-exclusive, irrevocable, fully paid-up license to such modifications and derivative works, and shall deliver such derivative works, including any source code, data, or information that pertains to such derivatives works, to Code 7630, U.S. Naval Research Laboratory. If any modifications to the Software substantially change the model output (including, but not limited to, alterations of the model formulation or model parameter values), then the modified Software shall not be identified “MSIS” without first obtaining the written consent of Counsel, Office of Naval Research, Department of the Navy. In such cases, the MSIS acronym shall nonetheless still appear in the individual files in order to document the provenance of the modified Software."*

- **Sub-Obligations Mapped to `orekit-nrlmsis21`**:
  1. **Prominent Change Notices & Dates**: Required on all modified files and derivative works. *(Upstream NRLMSIS 2.1 files were not modified; if `native/nrlmsis21_c_api.F90` or `native/nrlmsis21_jni.c` is deemed a derivative work, prominent provenance/change notices and dates must be added.)*
  2. **Mandatory Delivery to Authors / NRL Code 7630**: Any party who modifies the Software or creates a derivative work must deliver the modified portion or derivative work (including source code, data, or information pertaining to such derivative works) to NRL Code 7630. **Clarification Needed**: Which exact files (`nrlmsis21_c_api.F90`, `nrlmsis21_c_api.h`, `nrlmsis21_jni.c`, Java/Python adapters) must be delivered, and via what channel?
  3. **Public Open-Source Availability & U.S. Government License Grant**: Derivative works must be made public as open source and grant the U.S. Government a non-exclusive, irrevocable, fully paid-up license. **Clarification Needed**: Are there constraints on pairing an `Apache-2.0` candidate license for independently authored files with Section 4(b) obligations?
  4. **"Substantially Change the Model Output" Clause**: `Nrlmsis21Atmosphere.java` clamps altitudes `< 0.0 km` to `0.0 km` and returns `0.0 kg/m^3` above `maxAltitudeKm` (default `1000.0 km`), while leaving `0–1000 km` double-precision evaluations identical to upstream NRLMSIS 2.1. **Clarification Needed**: Does this Orekit domain boundary policy trigger the ONR Counsel written-consent requirement, or is it outside model formulation/parameter alteration?
