## 1. Purpose & Contractual Basis (`NRL-SOF-014-1`)

`orekit-nrlmsis21` integrates the U.S. Naval Research Laboratory (NRL) **NRLMSIS 2.1** empirical atmospheric model (`external/NRLMSIS2.1/*.F90` and `msis21.parm`) into the Java/Python Orekit astrodynamics ecosystem via a Fortran `ISO_C_BINDING` API (`native/nrlmsis21_c_api.F90`), a C JNI bridge (`native/nrlmsis21_jni.c`), Java Orekit `Atmosphere` / `SpaceWeatherProvider` adapters (`java/src/main/java/org/ssa/nrlmsis21/*.java`), and Python JPype bindings (`python/orekit_nrlmsis21/*.py`).

Under the **MSIS® (NRL-SOF-014-1) Open Source Academic Research License Agreement** (`external/NRLMSIS2.1/nrlmsis2.1_license.txt`):

- **Section 2 (Non-Commercial Only):** Use, reproduction, and modification are authorized solely for research, academic, and non-profit purposes. Any commercial use is prohibited.
- **Section 3 (Basis & Trademark):** MSIS® is property of the United States Government and a registered trademark of the Government of the United States of America, as represented by the Secretary of the Navy. Unauthorized use of the trademark is prohibited.
- **Section 4a (Sales & Data Products):** A user shall not sell, license, or transfer for a fee the Software or portion thereof, any derivative work, **or any data products generated by the Software**, without first obtaining written consent of IP Counsel for the Naval Research Laboratory.
- **Section 4b (Modifications, Open-Source Availability & Mandatory Delivery):**
  - All modifications and derivative works (including translations to other programming languages) shall carry prominent notices stating how the files were changed and the date of the change.
  - Any party who modifies the Software or creates a derivative work shall **deliver** the modified portion / derivative work (including source code, data, or information pertaining to such derivative works) to the authors or **NRL Code 7630, U.S. Naval Research Laboratory**.
  - Derivative works must be made available to the public as open-source software and grant the Government of the United States of America a non-exclusive, irrevocable, fully paid-up license.
  - If modifications substantially change the model output, the modified software shall not be identified as "MSIS" without written consent of Counsel, Office of Naval Research (ONR).
- **Section 4c (Notices):** Each copy or derivative work must include `nrlmsis2.1_license.txt` and the exact verbatim statement:
  > *"This software incorporates the MSIS® empirical atmospheric model software designed and provided by NRL. Use is governed by the Open Source Academic Research License Agreement contained in the file nrlmsis2.1_license.txt."*

## 3. Proposed Distribution Models Under Evaluation

In addition to evaluating combined academic distributions, this inquiry specifically evaluates a **Separate-Wrapper Distribution Model**:
- The public repository distributes **only** project-authored Java Orekit adapters (`java/`), Python bindings (`python/`), the C JNI bridge (`native/nrlmsis21_jni.c`), the Fortran `ISO_C_BINDING` C-API wrapper (`native/nrlmsis21_c_api.F90`, `native/nrlmsis21_c_api.h`), and integration/build scripts (`CMakeLists.txt`, `pom.xml`, `pyproject.toml`, `setup.py`).
- It **does not bundle** upstream NRLMSIS 2.1 Fortran source (`external/NRLMSIS2.1/*.F90`), parameter data (`msis21.parm`), or compiled NRLMSIS shared libraries (`.dll`/`.so`).
- End users independently obtain NRLMSIS 2.1 directly from NRL under the applicable license (`NRL-SOF-014-1` for academic/research use, or a separate commercial license from NRL), compile the upstream Fortran source locally, and link it to the integration wrapper.
